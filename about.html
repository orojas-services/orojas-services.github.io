About

I bring higher-education course development experience into instructional design.

I’m Orlando Rojas, an instructional designer and higher-education professional with experience supporting online course development, academic quality assurance, Canvas-based instruction, bilingual content, and learner-centered teaching.

Professional Background

Course quality, communication, and online learning

My professional background combines university teaching with several years of work supporting the development and review of online academic content.

I have collaborated with instructional designers, curriculum specialists, subject-matter experts, editors, quality assurance personnel, and academic leadership to review instructional materials, coordinate revisions, and support consistent course quality.

As a university instructor, I also work directly with Canvas, assessments, rubrics, learner activities, and student performance. That combination gives me experience with both course-production workflows and the learner-facing consequences of design decisions.

Current Focus

Instructional Design Canvas Online Learning Course Maintenance Quality Assurance Accessibility Assessment SME Collaboration Bilingual Content Learning Technologies

Approach

Instructional design as structured problem solving

I approach instructional design by first understanding the performance or learning need, the people involved, and the constraints of the environment.

From there, I focus on alignment among content, objectives, activities, assessments, technology, and learner support. I also value careful review, documentation, accessibility, and iteration because effective learning experiences depend on both sound design and consistent execution.

Where I'm Going

Expanding from higher education into Learning & Development

My next step is to apply this foundation more broadly in instructional design and Learning & Development roles, including online course maintenance, eLearning development, systems training, performance support, and workplace learning.
