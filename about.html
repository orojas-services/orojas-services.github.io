About

My work connects learning, language, and communication.

I am a bilingual educator, editor, and translator with more than five years of experience working with academic and instructional content in higher education. My work spans course development and quality review, English–Spanish editing and translation, and university teaching.

Professional Background

Working across courses, content, and learners

My experience has developed across different parts of the higher-education environment. I work with academic, curricular, and instructional materials; participate in course-development and review workflows; and teach undergraduate students in both face-to-face and online settings.

In content-development work, I collaborate with instructional designers, curriculum specialists, subject-matter experts, editors, and quality-assurance teams. My work includes reviewing and refining materials for language, terminology, coherence, accuracy, instructional consistency, and institutional standards.

In teaching, I work directly with course content, learner activities, assessment, feedback, rubrics, communication, and Canvas-supported learning. Together, these experiences have given me a perspective that connects the development of educational materials with the way learners actually encounter and use them.
