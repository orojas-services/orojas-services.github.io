About

Instructional design grounded in clarity, quality, and learner needs.

I’m Orlando Rojas, an instructional designer and higher education professional with experience supporting online course development, academic quality assurance, instructional content, and learner-centered teaching.

My background spans university teaching, academic editing and translation, Canvas course management, instructional materials development, and collaboration with instructional designers, curriculum specialists, subject-matter experts, and quality assurance teams.

I approach instructional design as a problem-solving process: understanding what learners need to do, identifying barriers to performance, selecting appropriate learning strategies and technologies, and improving the experience through review, feedback, and iteration.

I am especially interested in online learning, course quality, accessibility, learning technologies, and workplace learning. My goal is to bring the rigor and communication skills developed in higher education into corporate learning and development.
