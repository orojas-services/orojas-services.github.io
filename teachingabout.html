About My Teaching

My teaching connects communication, critical thinking, practice, and feedback.

I am a bilingual English–Spanish educator with experience teaching undergraduate English communication, rhetoric, and argumentation in online and face-to-face settings, complemented by nearly 450 hours of synchronous adult language instruction.

My broader professional background in academic content, editing, translation, curriculum review, and online course review also shapes how I think about clarity, instructional materials, assessment, and the learner experience.

Teaching Approach

Communication develops through purposeful use.

I approach communication as something students develop by doing: speaking, writing, analyzing, discussing, presenting, responding, and revising.

Rather than treating communication concepts only as abstract material, I use discussions, debates, role-playing, simulations, presentations, analytical writing, and other communicative tasks to give students opportunities to apply what they are learning.

These activities allow students to practice expressing ideas, developing positions, considering audiences, supporting claims, and communicating with greater clarity and confidence.

Rhetoric & Argumentation

Helping students make deliberate communication choices

A significant part of my current university teaching focuses on argumentation and rhetorical communication.

I guide students in examining how audience, purpose, rhetorical appeals, claims, evidence, and reasoning shape the effectiveness of spoken and written arguments.

The goal is not simply to identify rhetorical concepts, but to use them when analyzing communication and when constructing arguments of their own.

Assessment & Feedback

Clear expectations make feedback more useful.

I use assessments, rubrics, formative practice, and learner-progress monitoring to evaluate communication and make performance expectations visible.

Feedback should do more than explain a grade. I aim to identify specific strengths, problems, and areas for continued development so students can better understand how their communication is working and what they can improve next.

Online & LMS-Supported Teaching

Supporting learners across different environments

I teach in both online and face-to-face modalities and use Canvas to organize coursework, facilitate discussion, manage assignments and assessments, monitor progress, and provide rubric-based feedback.

I also use multimedia and communication tools to support synchronous, online, and multimodal learning.

Language Teaching Background

From adult language instruction to higher education

Before beginning my current university teaching, I worked as an English and Spanish language instructor with Berlitz Puerto Rico.

I delivered nearly 450 hours of synchronous online instruction to adult learners participating in employer-sponsored corporate language training programs.

That experience required adapting instruction to different proficiency levels, professional communication goals, and individual or group learning needs while using speaking activities, discussions, simulations, role-playing, progress monitoring, and targeted feedback.

Broader Professional Context

Teaching informed by academic content and course development

Alongside my teaching work, I have spent over five years working with academic, curricular, and instructional materials in higher education.

I collaborate with subject-matter experts, curriculum specialists, instructional designers, and quality-assurance teams to review and improve university courses and learning resources.

That work gives me an additional perspective on teaching: I regularly consider the relationship between course objectives, instructional content, activities, assessment, language, and what learners actually encounter.
