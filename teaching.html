Higher Education

Undergraduate English communication

As an Adjunct Professor of English Communication at Universidad del Sagrado Corazón, I teach students to develop and apply communication skills through speaking, writing, analysis, discussion, presentation, and argumentation.

My courses include work with rhetorical analysis, audience awareness, claim development, evidence-based reasoning, oral presentations, analytical and argumentative writing, discussion, debate, simulations, and other applied communication activities.

Assessment includes both formative and graded work, with clear criteria, rubric-based evaluation, and targeted feedback designed to make expectations and areas for development visible to students.

I use Canvas to organize coursework, manage assignments and assessments, facilitate online discussion, monitor learner progress, and provide feedback across online and face-to-face courses.

Additional Language Instruction

Adult English and Spanish teaching

Before moving into higher-education teaching, I worked as an English and Spanish language instructor with Berlitz Puerto Rico.

I delivered nearly 450 hours of synchronous online instruction to adult learners participating in employer-sponsored corporate language training programs.

The experience included individual and group instruction across different proficiency levels, with emphasis on professional communication, speaking practice, discussions, simulations, role-playing, progress monitoring, and targeted feedback.
