Higher Education

Undergraduate English communication

As an Adjunct Professor of English Communication at Universidad del Sagrado Corazón, I teach undergraduate communication courses in both online and face-to-face modalities.

My teaching includes oral presentations, analytical and argumentative writing, rhetorical analysis, audience awareness, claim development, evidence-based reasoning, discussion, and communication across academic and professional contexts.

I use learner-centered approaches such as discussions, role-playing, debates, simulations, and real-world communicative tasks to give students opportunities to apply concepts through active practice.
