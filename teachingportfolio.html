Teaching Methods

Communication develops through active use.

Discussion & Debate

I use structured discussions and debates to help students articulate positions, respond to others, support claims, and practice communicating ideas clearly.

Presentations

Oral presentations provide opportunities to develop organization, delivery, audience awareness, argumentation, and confidence in spoken English.

Role-Playing & Simulations

Role-playing, simulations, and real-world communicative tasks place language and communication skills within practical situations rather than treating them only as abstract concepts.

Analytical Writing

Written activities ask students to interpret material, develop claims, organize reasoning, use evidence, and communicate ideas coherently for a defined audience.

Rhetoric & Argumentation

Building arguments for real audiences

A significant part of my current teaching focuses on helping students understand how arguments function and how rhetorical choices change depending on audience, purpose, context, and evidence.

Students practice developing positions, explaining reasoning, evaluating rhetorical appeals, supporting claims with evidence, and communicating arguments through both written and oral forms.

Core Areas

Rhetorical Appeals Audience Awareness Claim Development Evidence Reasoning Analysis Persuasion

Assessment & Feedback

Making performance expectations visible

I use formative and graded assessments to evaluate student communication and monitor progress across speaking, writing, analysis, and course participation.

Rubrics help make expectations explicit, while feedback identifies specific strengths, weaknesses, and areas for continued development.

Rubric-Based Assessment

Performance criteria are used to evaluate communication consistently and connect grades to observable elements of student work.

Formative Assessment

Practice activities and intermediate assessments provide opportunities to identify problems before larger performance tasks.

Targeted Feedback

Feedback focuses on identifiable aspects of performance so students can understand what worked and what needs further development.

Progress Monitoring

LMS tools, assessment results, learner participation, and submitted work help me monitor progress throughout the course.

Online & LMS-Supported Teaching

Supporting learning beyond the physical classroom

I use Canvas to organize coursework, facilitate discussions, manage assignments and assessments, monitor student progress, and provide rubric-based feedback in online and face-to-face courses.

I also have experience with Moodle and with multimedia and communication tools used to support online, synchronous, and multimodal learning.
