Teaching in Practice

Communication develops through active use

My courses use discussion, presentation, writing, analysis, argumentation, and applied communication activities to give students opportunities to use course concepts rather than encounter them only as abstract material.

Discussion, Debate & Argumentation

Structured discussions and debates give students opportunities to articulate positions, respond to others, consider audience and purpose, support claims with evidence, and explain their reasoning.

Presentations & Oral Communication

Presentations and other speaking activities develop organization, delivery, audience awareness, confidence, and the ability to communicate ideas clearly in spoken English.

Analytical Writing & Applied Communication

Written work asks students to interpret material, develop claims, organize reasoning, use evidence, and communicate for defined audiences and purposes. Role-playing, simulations, and other applied tasks extend those skills into practical communication situations.

I teach in both online and face-to-face settings and use Canvas to organize coursework, manage assignments and assessments, facilitate discussion, monitor progress, and provide feedback.

Assessment & Feedback

Making expectations and progress visible

I use formative and graded assessments to evaluate student communication across speaking, writing, analysis, and course participation.

Rubrics & Assessment

Clear performance criteria connect grades to observable elements of student work and help make expectations visible before and during assessment.

Feedback & Progress Monitoring

Targeted feedback identifies specific strengths and areas for improvement, while assessment results, participation, submitted work, and Canvas activity help track progress throughout a course.

Adult Language Instruction

Nearly 450 hours of synchronous English and Spanish teaching

At Berlitz Puerto Rico, I delivered nearly 450 hours of synchronous online English and Spanish instruction to adult learners participating in employer-sponsored corporate language training programs.

I taught individuals and groups across different proficiency levels and professional communication goals, adapting activities, pacing, and feedback while using discussion, speaking practice, simulations, role-playing, and other communicative tasks.
