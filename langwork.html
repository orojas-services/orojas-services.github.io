The project required sustained work with culturally specific terminology, historical and social context, textual interpretation, translation decisions, and commentary for an English-language audience.

Professional Language Work

Editing, review, and translation of academic and instructional content

For more than five years, I have worked with academic, curricular, and instructional materials in higher education, editing, reviewing, and translating content across disciplines.

My work includes close review of language, terminology, coherence, accuracy, tone, consistency, and institutional requirements.

Much of this work is institutional or proprietary, so I do not reproduce client or university materials publicly. Instead, this portfolio describes the scope and type of work without exposing restricted content.

Working Approach

Meaning first, then clarity, consistency, and audience

Context

I consider the purpose, audience, subject matter, and communicative setting before making language decisions.

Terminology

I pay close attention to specialized terminology and its consistency across longer documents and related materials.

Clarity & Tone

I refine language so it communicates effectively without flattening the intended voice or changing the underlying meaning.

Review

I check the finished text for accuracy, coherence, consistency, and fit for its intended audience and context.
