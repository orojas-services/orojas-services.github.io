Document Quality

Academic standards, references, and consistency

My editorial work extends beyond sentence-level correction to document-level quality. I review references, citations, formatting, templates, institutional style requirements, and academic conventions while checking consistency across related materials.

I also perform bilingual quality review for terminology, clarity, coherence, linguistic accuracy, and alignment between English and Spanish content.

Collaborative Review

Language work within broader academic-development workflows

I work with subject-matter experts, curriculum specialists, instructional designers, quality-assurance personnel, and academic leadership to resolve content, terminology, and quality issues throughout development and review.

My work also includes incorporating review recommendations, coordinating revisions, and identifying potential copyright or intellectual-property concerns when they arise during content review.

Featured Public Translation

Tuna, as Told by and for Tunos

M.A. Thesis · Spanish → English · Translation & Research

My master's thesis, Tuna, as Told by and for Tunos, combined an English translation of Pedro J. Portal Basurco's La Tuna Contada por y para Tunos with research and an extended preface discussing translation theory, the concepts that informed the work, and the process used to produce the translation.

The project required sustained work with culturally specific terminology, textual interpretation, research, cultural context, and translation decisions for an English-language audience.

Unlike much of my professional editing and translation work, the thesis is publicly available and provides a direct example of my academic translation practice.
