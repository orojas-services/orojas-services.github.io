Case Overview

COM 210 — Professional Communication

A maintenance request was submitted for a live asynchronous online course after several instructional and technical inconsistencies were identified within one module.

The request required review of the affected course materials, identification of issues, coordination of appropriate corrections, accessibility and quality checks, documentation of changes, and clear communication of the final resolution.

Process

From maintenance request to QA sign-off

1. Review the Request

Examine the maintenance ticket, identify the affected course components, and determine the scope and urgency of the issue.

2. Diagnose the Issues

Review content, navigation, instructions, resources, accessibility, and alignment with the established course structure.

3. Coordinate and Correct

Determine which updates can be implemented directly and which require consultation with an SME, curriculum specialist, editor, administrator, or other stakeholder.

4. Perform QA

Verify the revised materials for functionality, consistency, accessibility, instructional clarity, and course integrity.

5. Document Changes

Record the issue, action taken, dependencies, review status, and final disposition for future reference.

6. Close the Request

Communicate the completed work clearly and identify any remaining actions or recommendations.
