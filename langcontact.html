Before You Write

Helpful project information

When possible, include the language direction, type of content, approximate scope, intended audience, and any relevant deadline or context.

For editing or document-review projects, it is also useful to mention any required style guide, formatting standard, terminology requirements, or institutional guidelines.

This helps me understand the purpose of the project and the kind of language, editorial, or quality review it may require.
