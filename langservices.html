Professional Experience

For more than five years, I have worked with academic, curricular, and instructional content in higher education, editing, reviewing, and translating materials across disciplines.

My work includes close attention to language, terminology, coherence, accuracy, institutional standards, and the way content communicates with its intended audience.

I also collaborate within broader course-development workflows involving instructional designers, curriculum specialists, subject-matter experts, editors, and quality-assurance teams.
