Professional Experience

Over five years in higher-education editing and translation

Since 2021, I have worked as an Editor/Translator supporting academic, curricular, and instructional content in English and Spanish.

My responsibilities include reviewing materials for linguistic accuracy, terminology, grammar, style, coherence, clarity, formatting, and alignment with institutional and accreditation standards.

I collaborate with subject-matter experts, curriculum specialists, instructional designers, quality-assurance personnel, editors, and academic leadership to resolve content, terminology, and quality issues throughout development and review workflows.

My work also includes incorporating review recommendations, supporting revision cycles, working with references and citations, and identifying potential copyright or intellectual-property concerns when they arise during content review.

I use CAT and digital collaboration tools, including Phrase and Microsoft 365, to support terminology consistency, bilingual review, and workflow efficiency.

Featured Work

Tuna, as Told by and for Tunos

M.A. Thesis · Spanish → English · Translation & Research

My master's thesis, Tuna, as Told by and for Tunos, combined an English translation of Pedro J. Portal Basurco's La Tuna Contada por y para Tunos with research and an extended preface discussing translation theory, the concepts that informed the work, and the process used to produce the translation.

The project required sustained attention to terminology, textual analysis, cultural context, interpretation, and the communication of a culturally specific subject for an English-language audience.
