My work combines language, editing, translation, and academic content.

I am a bilingual English–Spanish editor and translator with over five years of experience reviewing, refining, and translating academic, curricular, instructional, and specialized content.

My professional background combines higher-education content development with graduate training in translation, language, writing, editing, proofreading, terminology, and specialized translation.

Professional Background

Editing and translation within higher education

Since 2021, I have worked as an Editor/Translator supporting academic, curricular, and instructional materials in English and Spanish.

My work includes reviewing language at both the sentence and document level for grammar, orthography, syntax, style, terminology, coherence, clarity, formatting, consistency, and overall editorial quality.

I also review references, citations, institutional style requirements, and academic conventions while helping ensure that content meets established quality requirements.

This work takes place within multidisciplinary academic-development environments. I collaborate with subject-matter experts, curriculum specialists, instructional designers, quality-assurance personnel, and academic leadership to resolve language, content, terminology, and quality issues throughout review and revision workflows.

Approach

Meaning first, followed by clarity, precision, and consistency.

I begin by considering what the text needs to communicate, who needs to understand it, and what linguistic, disciplinary, or cultural context shapes its meaning.

From there, I focus on accuracy, terminology, syntax, clarity, coherence, tone, style, and consistency while preserving the intended meaning and communicative purpose of the content.

For academic and instructional materials, I also consider how language choices interact with structure, references, formatting, institutional standards, and the broader learner or reader experience.

Graduate Education

Master of Arts in Translation

University of Puerto Rico, Río Piedras Campus · December 2024

I earned my Master of Arts in Translation from the University of Puerto Rico, Río Piedras Campus in December 2024, completing the degree with a 3.60 GPA.

My graduate preparation combined translation practice with advanced study of language, writing, editing, syntax, semiotics, specialized translation, and computer-assisted translation tools. The program included work across legal, financial, media, journalistic, museum, and other specialized translation contexts.

Graduate Research

Tuna, as Told by and for Tunos

M.A. Thesis · Spanish → English · Translation & Research

My master's thesis, Tuna, as Told by and for Tunos, combined an English translation of Pedro J. Portal Basurco's La Tuna Contada por y para Tunos with research and an extended preface discussing translation theory, the concepts that informed the work, and the process used to produce the translation.

The project required sustained work with culturally specific terminology, textual interpretation, research, cultural context, and translation decisions for an English-language audience.
