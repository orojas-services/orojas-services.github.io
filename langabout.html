About

My work combines language, editing, translation, and academic content.

I am a bilingual English–Spanish editor and translator with over five years of experience reviewing, refining, and translating academic, curricular, instructional, and specialized content.

My professional background combines higher-education content development with graduate training in translation, advanced language study, writing, editing, proofreading, terminology, and specialized translation.

Professional Background

Editing and translation within higher education

Since 2021, I have worked as an Editor/Translator supporting academic, curricular, and instructional materials in English and Spanish.

My work includes reviewing language at both the sentence and document level for grammar, orthography, syntax, style, terminology, coherence, clarity, formatting, consistency, and overall editorial quality.

I also review references, citations, institutional style requirements, and academic conventions while helping ensure that content meets established quality and compliance standards.

Collaborative Practice

Language work as part of a broader review process

My work takes place within multidisciplinary academic-development environments rather than in isolation.

I collaborate with subject-matter experts, curriculum specialists, instructional designers, quality-assurance personnel, and academic leadership to resolve language, content, terminology, and quality issues.

This experience has made collaboration, revision, consistency, and communication central parts of my professional editing and translation practice.

Approach

Meaning first, followed by clarity, precision, and consistency.

I begin by considering what the text needs to communicate, who needs to understand it, and what linguistic, disciplinary, or cultural context shapes its meaning.

From there, I focus on accuracy, terminology, syntax, clarity, coherence, tone, style, and consistency while preserving the intended meaning and communicative purpose of the content.

For academic and instructional materials, I also consider how language choices interact with structure, references, formatting, institutional standards, and the broader learner or reader experience.
